Where do you deliver?+

Across Southern Ontario, including Hamilton, Brantford, Waterloo Region, Guelph, Niagara, and the Greater Toronto Area. Include your city in your enquiry so we can discuss delivery.

Do I have to pay with Bitcoin?+

Bitcoin is preferred, but cash, e-transfer, and online payments are also accepted.

How does ordering work?+

Choose a beef box, quarter, or half and get in touch. We’ll reply with availability and next steps, then work with you to arrange delivery.
